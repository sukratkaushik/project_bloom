A4 Sheet 3 of 4 • Partner Co-Pilot

The Partner / Spouse Companion Experience

Direct WebRTC P2P Sync, Maternal Privacy Sovereignty & Active Birth Readiness

Mother's Phone (Controller)

  • Encrypted P2P WebRTC Pairing: Connected directly via temporary 6-digit sync code or QR standee without intermediate cloud sniffing.
  • Granular Consent Checkboxes: The mother explicitly chooses which categories to share:
    • [x] Hospital Logistics & Assigned Tasks
    • [x] Fetal Kick Counter Sessions
    • [x] Contraction Timing & Emergency Alerts
  • Intimacy & Dignity Safeguard: Her raw emotional journal, personal symptom details, and private notes stay completely confidential unless she toggles them on.
  • Instant Access Revocation: The mother can sever the partner pairing at any moment with 1 tap in Profile.

Partner's Phone (Co-Pilot)

  • Dedicated Non-Pregnant View: Streamlined UI focused on practical support, avoiding redundant maternal tracking forms.
  • Actionable Logistics Checklist:
    • "Keep minimum ₹5,000 cash ready in wallet for hospital admission deposits."
    • "Test-drive route to hospital during peak morning and evening traffic."
    • "Pack extra power banks, warm wrap, and snacks for labor room."
  • Bonding Through Vitals: Real-time mirror of baby kick counts and milestone progress keeps the spouse emotionally engaged.
  • Zero Medical Misinformation: Accesses verified clinical facts without unvetted internet panic.
🚨

Simultaneous 5-1-1 Active Labor Alert

When the mother logs contractions that fulfill the clinical 5-1-1 rule (5 mins apart, lasting 60 seconds, for 1 full hour), the partner’s phone sounds a high-priority notification: "Active labor pattern detected. Start the car and call your obstetrician now."
