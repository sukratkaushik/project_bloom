## Slide 1: Executive Overview & The Problem

### The Challenge Facing Leading Maternity Hospital Chains
While tier-1 hospital chains deliver world-class clinical care during labor, outpatient prenatal monitoring between scheduled visits remains fragmented, chaotic, and unmonetized:

1. **Chaotic Outpatient Communication:** Doctors and duty nurses are overwhelmed with frantic WhatsApp messages, blurry photos of test results, and vague symptom reports at 2 AM.
2. **Consultation Inefficiency:** Obstetricians spend 4–6 minutes of every 15-minute consultation deciphering handwritten symptom diaries, hunting for blood pressure trends, or asking repetitive kick-count questions.
3. **Missed Early Warning Signs:** Preeclampsia (BP spikes) and fetal distress (reduced kick velocity) often escalate unnoticed between prenatal visits, leading to preventable emergency admissions.
4. **Patient Churn & Leaked Ancillary Revenue:** Patients frequently book delivery packages elsewhere if they don't feel continuously connected to the hospital brand, while spending thousands on maternity retail and pharmacy outside the hospital network.

## Slide 3: The 90-Second Clinical Triage Portal (For Doctors)

### Built Specifically for Obstetricians — Not a Patient Social Feed
Doctors will never log kick sessions or water intake. The OPIN Doctor Portal is engineered strictly for rapid clinical risk stratification:

### 1. Active Patient Roster (Automated Urgency Triage)
* 🔴 **High Alert:** Blood Pressure $\ge 140/90\text{ mmHg}$, reduced fetal movement ($<10$ kicks in 2 hours), or active labor indicators (5-1-1 contraction rule).
* 🟡 **Watch List:** Approaching due date ($\ge 38$ weeks), high-risk factors (IVF, twin gestation, gestational diabetes), or missed vital logs.
* 🟢 **Routine:** Gestation progressing within normal physiological limits.

### 2. Single-Patient 90-Second Clinical Summary
* **Gestational Age & EDD:** Live calculation (e.g., Week 31 + 2 days) with associated risk tags.
* **Vitals & Trends:** Systolic/Diastolic BP graphs over time with pre-eclampsia thresholds.
* **Fetal Activity:** Daily kick count trends and duration curves.
* **Labor Readiness:** Real-time contraction frequency, duration, and interval tracking.
* **Interoperability:** Instant 1-click export to **HL7 / FHIR R4 JSON** bundles or printable clinical PDF summaries for the hospital EHR.

## Slide 4: Clinical Governance & Risk Mitigation

### Moving from Reactive Care to Proactive Outpatient Monitoring

| Risk Factor | Current Standard Practice | With OPIN Enterprise Platform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pre-eclampsia Detection** | Checked only during monthly/bi-weekly in-clinic consultations. | Patients log daily home BP; portal automatically flags $\ge 140/90\text{ mmHg}$ trends for immediate clinical outreach. |
| **Fetal Distress & Stillbirth Prevention** | Relies on patient memory: *"Baby felt less active yesterday."* | Formal Cardiff kick counter logs kick intervals; triggers alert if baby fails 10 kicks in 2 hours. |
| **Labor Triage Overhead** | Panicked midnight calls to on-call doctors for Braxton Hicks contractions. | Offline contraction timer applies the clinical **5-1-1 rule** (contractions 5 mins apart, lasting 1 min, for 1 hour) before advising hospital admission. |
| **Clinical Documentation** | Manual transcription of home readings into hospital notes. | 1-Click structured FHIR export into existing Hospital Information Systems (HIS). |

## Slide 7: Patient Data Sovereignty & Indian Compliance

### Enterprise Healthcare Standards Built-In

* **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) Compliant:** Clear consent architecture, patient-owned data, right to erasure, and zero unauthorized commercial harvesting of medical records.
* **PCPNDT Compliance:** Strict architectural adherence to Indian reproductive healthcare regulations (zero prenatal gender-determination features or search indexing).
* **Clinical Health Records Are Never Brokered:** We draw a strict line between secure, confidential medical vitals (BP, kick logs, ultrasound summaries) and commercial marketplace placements.
* **Local-First Architecture:** Sensitive vitals are stored on-device (IndexedDB) and shared with the hospital via end-to-end encrypted tunnels.
