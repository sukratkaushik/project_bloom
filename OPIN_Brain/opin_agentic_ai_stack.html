🌸 Our Pregnancy (OPIN)

Maternal Agentic AI

🔒 1. Local-First Clinical Privacy

Unlike standard cloud RAG setups that transmit sensitive patient telemetry to third parties, OPIN tracks kicks, contractions, vitals, and nutrition inside Dexie.js (IndexedDB) on the mother's device with zero mandatory cloud sync.

Local First Zero Cloud Storage WebRTC P2P Sync

🛡️ 2. Clinical Triage & Guardrails

Strict FOGSI (India) and ACOG compliance. Heuristic engines immediately intercept high-risk maternal symptoms (e.g. BP $\ge$ 140/90 mmHg, 5-1-1 labor rule) before forwarding to LLM reasoning.

FOGSI Guidelines ACOG Compliant Emergency Triage
