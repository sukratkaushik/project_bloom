# OPIN Company Context & Strategic Vision

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
* **Brand Name:** Bloom (operating as **Our Pregnancy** / **OPIN**)
* **Domain:** [ourpregnancy.in](https://ourpregnancy.in)
* **Tagline:** Your privacy-first, local-first pregnancy companion.
* **Core Philosophy:** "Your pregnancy health data belongs to you, not advertising brokers."

## 2. The Core Problem We Solve
Traditional pregnancy apps harvest intimate maternal data, serve aggressive ads, crash without cell reception in hospital wards, and fail to connect with actual doctors. 

Bloom solves this by combining:
1. **Local-First Data Sovereignty:** Core vitals and logs live on the device (IndexedDB) with zero required server roundtrips.
2. **Offline-First Functionality:** Full access to kick counters, contraction timers, and birth plans even in basement labor rooms.
3. **Clinical Interoperability:** Data is mapped to **HL7 / FHIR R4** standards for seamless export to doctors and hospitals.

## 3. The Dual B2C & B2B2C Model
Instead of choosing between pregnant mothers (B2C) and healthcare institutions (B2B):
* **The Product:** A consumer-friendly, calming, comprehensive pregnancy companion app for expectant parents.
* **The Distribution (B2B2C):** Independent private OB-GYN clinics and doctors in India act as primary distribution partners.
  * **Doctor's Pain:** Patients show up with disorganized notes or send panicked WhatsApp messages at 2 AM.
  * **The Offer:** A desktop QR standee. Doctors invite patients using a 90-day VIP access code (`VIPCARE90`). Patients link their vitals to the doctor's 90-second clinical triage portal.
  * **Result:** Zero customer acquisition cost (CAC), instant patient trust, and doctor workflow optimization.

## 4. Target Personas
1. **The Expectant Mother (B2C Primary):** Seeks anxiety relief, week-by-week development guides, kick counters, contraction tracking, and dietary reassurance without intrusive ads.
2. **The Partner / Co-Parent:** Uses peer-to-peer (WebRTC) sync to share hospital checklists, birth plans, and vitals without central server routing.
3. **The Private OB-GYN (B2B2C Partner):** Independent practitioners with high daily patient volumes who need a 90-second glance at patient BP, kicks, and labor readiness before physical exams.

## 5. Geographic Roadmap
* **Phase 1 (Immediate - India Tier 1 Cities):** Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai. Target standalone private maternity clinics (1–3 doctor setups). High smartphone adoption, doctor autonomy, and rapid decision-making.
* **Phase 2 (Global Expansion):** US, UK, EU, UAE. Emphasize **Data Privacy & Reproductive Sovereignty** (especially post-Roe US market) as a direct-to-consumer privacy play.
