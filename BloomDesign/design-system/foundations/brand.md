# Brand foundation

## Who we are

**Our Pregnancy** is a privacy-first pregnancy companion app made for Indian mothers. We exist because the average pregnancy app is built for a Western audience, treats users as data points, and charges for tools that should be free.

## Mission

Give every expectant mother in India a calm, well-designed, free, privacy-first companion through pregnancy — one that respects both modern medicine and the cultural rhythms of life in India.

## Vision

A healthy, joyful, anxiety-free pregnancy journey for every Indian mother, without the cost of an app subscription or the noise of clinical-feeling tools.

## Archetype

**The Caregiver × The Sage.** Empathetic, nurturing, knowledgeable, deeply trusted. Think a thoughtful older sister who happens to be a doctor — calm, kind, knows the science, but speaks human.

## Pillars

1. **Privacy-first.** Local-first data. Cloud sync is opt-in. No data brokering, no ads.
2. **Free forever (core).** Vitals, kicks, contractions, birth plan, government schemes — free for everyone, always.
3. **Indian by design.** Built around Indian foods, schemes, helplines, festivals, family structure.
4. **Calm interfaces.** No alarm-red. No hype. Sandalwood backgrounds. Soft motion. Calm Mode for 3am.

## Tagline & elevator pitch

**Tagline:** *Your free pregnancy companion — secure & synced.*

**Elevator pitch (50 words):**
> Our Pregnancy is a privacy-first pregnancy companion built for Indian mothers. Track vitals, count kicks, time contractions, plan the hospital bag, and navigate JSY and PMMVY — entirely free. Your data stays on your device. The interface is calm, the language is warm, and the cultural context is yours.

## Anti-brand

We are **not**:
- A clinical / medical-looking app.
- A US/EU pregnancy app translated to Indian English.
- An "AI-powered" hype product.
- An ad-supported or data-monetized app.
- A subscription wall around basic safety features.
