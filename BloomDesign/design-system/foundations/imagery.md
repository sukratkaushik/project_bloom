# Imagery & illustration

## Photography direction

### Subjects

- **Real Indian women** — South Asian features, ages 22–35, expecting.
- **Comfortable everyday wear** — soft cotton kurtas, light sarees, easy shirts, salwar kameez. NOT business attire, NOT hospital gowns, NOT athleisure-as-uniform.
- **Comfortable real spaces** — home interiors (warm wood, plants, soft light), gardens, balconies, kitchens, neighborhood streets in daylight. NOT studio-white backdrops, NOT corporate offices, NOT generic "wellness" gyms.
- **Partner / family** — when a second person is shown, it's a partner or mom/MIL, never a friend group. The brand serves the relational unit, not the social influencer.

### Lighting

- **Warm morning or golden-hour light.** Calm, peaceful, well-rested look.
- **Soft, diffused.** No harsh midday shadows.
- **Indoor: large windows.** Outdoor: shade or backlit golden hour.

### Mood

- Joyful, present, calm. Not glamorous, not melancholic, not "fierce mom" empowerment.
- Hands resting on belly is fine. Yoga poses are fine. Birth-ball at home is fine.
- A pregnant woman laughing with her partner at the kitchen table = on-brand.
- A pregnant woman in a hospital gown next to medical equipment = off-brand.

### What we never show

- ❌ **Stock "diverse team around a laptop"** — generic corporate.
- ❌ **AI-generated humans** — slightly off, uncanny, ethically questionable for a maternal brand.
- ❌ **Hospital / clinical settings** — beds, monitors, IVs, gloves, scrubs.
- ❌ **Medical instruments** — speculums, stethoscopes, BP cuffs centered as imagery.
- ❌ **Blood, needles, cervix diagrams** — anything that signals "medical alarm."
- ❌ **Western-only contexts** — strollers in NYC parks, hospital deliveries in white US settings.
- ❌ **Glamour / styled bumps** — black backdrop maternity studio shoots, lace robes, etc.
- ❌ **Babies as commodity** — strollers, branded baby gear, "must-have" registries.

## Illustration direction

When photography won't work (concept illustrations, empty states, hero ornaments), use **flat vector illustration** with these traits:

- **Soft curves.** No sharp corners, no harsh geometry.
- **Rounded characters.** If a person is drawn, soft body proportions, round head, gentle features.
- **Limited palette.** Use the brand colors, never introduce new hues.
- **Line weights:** 2px for primary strokes, 1px for secondary. Never hairline.
- **No gradients on illustrations** — flat fills only, except very subtle 2-stop pastels on backgrounds.
- **No "AI-tech" tropes** — no glowing orbs, neural meshes, wireframe globes, holograms, digital sparkles.

### Style references that fit

- The lotus logo aesthetic — soft watercolor-flat blend, organic shapes.
- Mehndi (henna) line-art for decorative elements.
- Botanical line drawings (a sprig of tulsi, a curling vine) as subtle borders.
- Editorial illustrations from women's-health editorial design — *not* tech/SaaS marketing illustration.

### Style references that DON'T fit

- 3D / claymation / blob-bodies style (Mailchimp / Notion era).
- Memphis design / squiggles / 80s pop.
- Cyberpunk / neon / glitch.
- Anything resembling Apple-style flat-with-gradient app icon style.

## The shipped baby-size visual

The dashboard shows the baby's current size as an **organic emoji-style render** (Week 9 = brain emoji 🧠; later weeks = fruit/vegetable). Keep this. Do not replace with realistic foetal renders — that would push the brand clinical.

## Brand patterns

In `assets/patterns/`:

- **mehndi-watermark.svg** — Mehndi line-art motif for subtle card backgrounds. Use at 6–10% opacity.
- **rangoli-corner.svg** — Rangoli geometric corner ornament for decks and posters.
- **grid.svg** — Soft 24px dotted Sage grid for technical / data-heavy surfaces.
- **brand-wash.svg** — Sandalwood-to-mint vertical gradient wash for full-bleed marketing.

Use patterns as **background watermarks**, never as a foreground element.

## Indian context cues in imagery

When you need to signal "made for India" visually:

- A jharokha-style window in the background.
- A plant — money plant, tulsi, marigold.
- A soft cotton dupatta over the shoulder.
- A bowl of dal / cup of chai on the table.
- A bindi (subtle, never the focal point).
- Daylight through a balcony grill.

Avoid clichés: Taj Mahal silhouettes, lotus pond stock photos, henna-hands-only crops, "spiritual" mandala kitsch.
