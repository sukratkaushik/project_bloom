# Templates

This folder holds the **canonical visual reference** for every surface Our Pregnancy ships.

## Real vs. synthetic — read first

When you're picking a template to copy or adapt:

- **Real shipped assets** (clearly labeled) are the source of truth. Use these whenever possible.
- **Synthetic drafts** (clearly labeled) were generated by the design-system-creator and are **pending review**. Flag them with your team — every synthetic should be replaced with a real shipped version on the next pass.
