# Social — Instagram

Our primary social channel. Audience: expectant Indian women, 22–35, mostly tier-1/2 cities, smartphone-native. Tone: warm, specific, photographable.

## Formats and sizes

| Format | Dimensions | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed post (portrait)** | 1080 × 1350 | Default — knowledge tips, week-by-week, testimonials |
| **Feed post (square)** | 1080 × 1080 | Quote cards, fast announcements |
| **Story** | 1080 × 1920 | Day-in-life, polls, behind-the-scenes |
| **Reel cover** | 1080 × 1920 | Video cover frame — same Story canvas |
| **Carousel cover** | 1080 × 1350 | First card sets the hook for swipe-through content |
| **Carousel inner** | 1080 × 1350 | Same canvas, content varies per card |

## Layout system

### Feed post (portrait 1080×1350) — the workhorse

| Zone | Content | Visual rules |
|---|---|---|
| Top 12% | Brand mark — small lotus + "Our Pregnancy" wordmark, Charcoal | Mark 64px tall, wordmark Playfair italic 28pt |
| Middle 70% | Main content — quote / question / illustration / photo | Playfair Display headline up to 60pt, sentence case |
| Bottom 18% | Tagline strip or footer — *ourpregnancy.in* | Nunito 18pt, Medium color |

### Story (1080×1920) — flow with the brand

Same hierarchy as feed, but vertical breathing room. Title can go up to 80pt Playfair.

### Carousel (10-card series — common formats)

1. **Cover** — Hook question or claim. Playfair 60pt, swipe arrow at bottom-right.
2. **Cards 2-9** — One idea per card. Either:
   - Question + answer pattern (card N has question, card N+1 has answer)
   - Numbered tip pattern (1/8, 2/8, …) — small footer "X/Y" indicator
3. **Final card** — CTA: "Save this 🤍 or send it to a friend who's expecting" + lotus mark.

## Caption rules

- **First line earns the tap** — punchy, no fluff. Saying *"Folate in the first trimester"* beats *"Did you know?? 🤰"*.
- **Body** — 1–3 short paragraphs. White space between. Each ending in a period (not three dots).
- **CTA line** — soft, specific. *"Save this 🤍 if you found it useful"* or *"Send to a friend who's expecting"*.
- **Hashtags** — bottom of caption, max 6, no spam columns. Approved set:
  > #ourpregnancy #pregnancy #pregnancyindia #pregnancyjourney #pregnantinindia #motherhood
- **Emoji** — sparing. Approved: 🌸 ✨ 🤍 🌿 🇮🇳 🤰🏻. Never: 🚨 ⚠️ 💊 ❗.
- **Always include the disclaimer** in any health-fact caption: *Always consult your OB or midwife.*

## What we never post

- Stock photos of "diverse moms in white-room studio."
- AI-generated humans.
- Memes that aren't 100% on-brand (no "moms-just-need-coffee" content).
- Stats without sources (cite WHO, NHS, or the Govt of India scheme name).
- Anything clinical-looking — hospital photos, stethoscopes, IV drips.
- Engagement-bait ("comment below if you're pregnant!").
