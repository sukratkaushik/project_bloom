# Social — LinkedIn

Audience: founders, healthcare professionals, gynaecologists, investors, government / public-health folks, prospective hires. Tone: thoughtful, specific, never marketing-speak.

## Posting cadence

- **1–2 long-form text posts per week** (founder voice, no images required).
- **1 carousel or single graphic per week** (product update, customer story, milestone).
- **No daily posting.** Quality > frequency. The audience is professional.

## The voice on LinkedIn

LinkedIn voice = brand voice + one extra notch of **specificity**.

- Open with one specific observation, not a claim.
- Number every claim. ("We have 1,247 weekly active users at week 6, 60% from Tier-2/3 cities.")
- Show the work. Build-in-public over polished marketing.
- Sign with first name + role. Never "the team" generically.

### Good first-line openers

- *We launched Our Pregnancy 6 weeks ago. Here's what we learned.*
- *A user emailed yesterday: "Your kick counter saved me a hospital trip." Here's the context.*
- *India has 27M pregnancies a year. Most pregnancy apps are built for the US. So we built one for here.*
- *Three things I'd do differently if I started Our Pregnancy again.*

### Avoid

- *Excited to announce…*
- *Thrilled to share…*
- *Pleased to introduce…*
- *Honoured to be featured…*
- Three-line "build suspense" intros with line breaks.
- Engagement bait ("Agree? Comment below.").

## Single image post — layout

| Zone | Content |
|---|---|
| Top-left | Small horizontal lockup (lotus + wordmark), Charcoal on Sandalwood |
| Center | Main element — pull quote, stat, screenshot, or photo |
| Bottom-right | `ourpregnancy.in` URL in Nunito 16pt Medium |

**1200×627 templates for re-use:**

1. **Pull-quote card** — Playfair 48pt italic quote, Sage-light quote mark.
2. **Stat card** — One Playfair 100pt number, Nunito 22pt label below, optional 14pt source.
3. **Screenshot card** — Phone mock 60% width on left, headline + 2 bullets right.
4. **Photo card** — Real photograph 60% width left, 40% right has Playfair headline + Nunito body.

## Carousel PDF — 8-card thread

Common structure:

1. **Cover** — Hook headline + small lotus mark + "swipe →" prompt at bottom.
2. **Setup** — Context for the story.
3-7. **Body** — One point per card. Use Playfair for headers, Nunito for body.
8. **CTA** — Specific next step (read the blog, try the app, DM founder).

## Carousel content ideas

- *6 things we learned launching a pregnancy app in India.*
- *Why we're free forever for the basics.*
- *The 4 government schemes every Indian mother should know.*
- *Why we don't ask for an account on signup.*
- *Local-first vs cloud-first: how we built sync that respects privacy.*

## Hashtags

Use 3–5 hashtags max, at the bottom of the post (not in body). Approved set:

> #womenshealth #healthtech #digitalhealth #india #maternalhealth #buildinpublic #ourpregnancy

Never use: #motivation #entrepreneurship #leadership (generic).
